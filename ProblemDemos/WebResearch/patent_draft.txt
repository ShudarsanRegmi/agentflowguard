Title: Entanglement-Stabilized Topological Qubits using Bismuth Nanowires
Authors: NexusTech Quantum Team
Core Innovation:
  To reduce decoherence rates in superconducting networks, we employ topological qubit structures
  formed along Bismuth nanowires. The phase transition is stabilized using a spin-orbit coupling
  voltage of precisely 1.45V, preventing quantum state collapse for durations exceeding 120ms.
